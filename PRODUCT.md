## Platform

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS on GitHub Pages, no build step (existing repo convention).

## Users

Owners and managers of cafés, restaurants, bakeries and dessert shops in Oman (inferred from the site's Omani Arabic copy, OMR prices and +968 WhatsApp number). They discover the offer on TikTok/Instagram or a shared link, usually on a phone, and decide whether to message on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Ahmed Al-Wahaibi (منيو عُمان) designs interactive QR menus: the diner scans a table code and sees large dish photos that animate on tap; optionally the same menu runs as a looping display on an in-store screen. This is a side income; success = more qualified WhatsApp enquiries that name a style and a package.

## Positioning

A hand-made, animated poster-style menu (dish swaps, spins, colour shifts, type reveals) instead of a PDF or a generic menu app, made by one Omani designer, ordered and changed over WhatsApp. Real proof available: the Nana Tea (نعنع) menu and screen at /nana/.

## Offer

Packages: QR menu; QR + in-store screen; with ongoing updates. Add-ons: bilingual AR/EN, WhatsApp ordering, social/map links, printable table card. Prices: owner will supply (OPEN).

## Voice

Gulf/Omani colloquial Arabic, first person, short and direct (e.g. «أصمم لمطعمك منيو يتحرك.»).
