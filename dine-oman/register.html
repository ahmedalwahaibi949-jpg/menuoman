Questions

Before you apply

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. Applying is free, and the Basic package stays free. Paid packages are only confirmed after approval.

Who can register?

Restaurants, cafés, bakeries, dessert shops, food trucks and licensed home kitchens operating in Oman.

How will I know I'm approved?

We email you as soon as our team reviews your application. You can also sign in with your email at any time to see its status.

I have more than one branch.

Submit one application per branch, so each has its own location, hours and page.

Can I change my details later?

Yes. After approval you can update hours, menu, photos and links from your owner account whenever you need to.

Can I apply in Arabic?

Yes. Write your name and description in Arabic or English. After approval you can add both.
