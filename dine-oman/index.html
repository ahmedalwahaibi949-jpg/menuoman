Dine Oman is where people in Oman, and the people visiting, decide where to eat. This book sets out how the brand looks, how it speaks, and how it launches to diners and to restaurant owners.

Recommended mark · The Arch D
00 · Brand foundation

One place to answer “where shall we eat?”

Oman's food scene is spread across Instagram accounts, WhatsApp forwards, map pins and word of mouth. Dine Oman pulls it into one trusted place: every restaurant, café, bakery and food business, from Muscat to Salalah.

Positioning statement

For residents and visitors in Oman who want to find a good place to eat, Dine Oman is the restaurant discovery platform that lists every restaurant in one destination, with honest information, real menus and local knowledge, so choosing is quick and confident.

داين عُمان هو المكان اللي يجمع كل مطاعم وكافيهات ومخابز عُمان في وجهة وحدة، بمعلومات واضحة ومنيوهات حقيقية، عشان تختار وين تاكل بسرعة وبثقة.

Two audiences, one promise
Diners
Residents, families, students, office workers and tourists. Promise: find the right place fast.
Owners
Restaurants, cafés, bakeries, home kitchens, food trucks. Promise: be found by people already deciding.
Shared
Trust. Accurate hours, prices, menus and locations, kept current.

Personality

Generous

Like an Omani host. Shows the full table, never just the paid seats.

Trustworthy

Accurate details, clear labels on anything sponsored, no inflated claims.

Local

Knows the difference between Al Khuwair and Al Khoud, and says shuwa, not “slow-roast lamb”.

Modern

Clean, quick, phone-first. Premium through restraint, not gold foil.

Voice

We say

  • “Open now in Ruwi: 14 places, 3 serve breakfast till noon.”
  • جوعان؟ شوف إيش مفتوح قريب منك.
  • “List your café free. It takes ten minutes.”

We avoid

  • “The ultimate world-class culinary experience awaits!”
  • “Oman's #1 platform” (until we can prove it)
  • Formal MSA in social captions; Gulf slang that excludes visitors

Arabic: Omani-friendly colloquial for social and ads, clear Modern Standard Arabic for the app interface and legal text. English: plain, warm, short sentences. Every key asset ships in both.

01 · Logo concepts

Three marks, one recommendation

Each concept turns the idea of “one destination” into a shape that works at app-icon size and on a shop window sticker.

A · The Arch D

Recommended

A bold “D” whose counter is the pointed arch found on Omani forts, doors and mosque windows. The saffron dot is the lamp in the doorway: the destination.

Ownable, local without cliché, reads as a doorway you walk through to eat. Strong as an app icon and favicon.

B · Pin & Plate

Alternative

A map pin whose head is a plate seen from above. Says “restaurant discovery” in one glance.

Instantly understood, but pins are common in maps and delivery apps, so it is harder to protect and to stand apart.

C · Eight-Point Star

Alternative

The khatam star from Omani carving and silverwork. Eight points for every direction across the country, meeting at one centre.

Rich and heritage-led. Works best as a secondary pattern, which is how the system uses it.

Arch D lockups

Horizontal for headers and ads, stacked for profile images and posters, icon alone for the app.

Clear space

Keep space equal to the height of the arch's saffron dot ×2 on every side.

Minimum size

Icon 16 px digital / 6 mm print. Horizontal lockup 96 px / 25 mm wide.

Don't

Recolour the dot, outline the mark, stretch it, or set the Arabic name in a different face from Reem Kufi.

Arabic name. “داين عُمان” is a direct transliteration, which keeps search and word of mouth consistent across both languages. If a fully Arabic name is preferred, “مائدة عُمان” (Oman's table) is a strong alternative. Check trademark availability and the dineoman domain and handles before final artwork.

09 · Launch campaign

“Every restaurant. One destination.”

Supply comes before demand: launch to restaurants first so diners find a full platform on day one. The phases below run in order.

Supply buildWeeks −8 to −3

Sign Founding Restaurants through field visits and WhatsApp in Muscat first, then Sohar, Nizwa, Sur and Salalah. Photograph menus and dishes. Give each founder a window sticker and a launch-day feature.

  • Owner ads (chapter 07) and Owner POV TikToks
  • Reserve handles, set up WhatsApp Business, landing page with “list free” form
TeaserWeeks −2 to −1

Mystery campaign in Arabic: «وين ناكل؟ قريب، كل الجواب في مكان واحد.» Grid of blurred dishes and a countdown. Waitlist sign-up for early access.

  • Creators post “I can't tell you yet” teasers
  • Founding restaurants hint at it in their own Stories
Launch weekWeek 0

Reveal film (30 s and 15 s): a montage moving across Oman, a mountain café, a Muttrah fish grill, a Salalah coconut stand, a Muscat bakery at dawn, each a door that opens into the next, ending on the Arch D and the line “Every restaurant. One destination.”

  • Launch event or food night with founding restaurants, press and creators
  • Each founding restaurant gets its “New on Dine Oman” post the same day
  • Outdoor in Muscat (Sultan Qaboos Street, malls) if budget allows
  • Press release to Times of Oman, Muscat Daily, Oman Observer, Al Watan, Atheer
MomentumWeeks 1 to 6

Turn on the always-on “Where shall we eat?” campaign and the TikTok series. Start the Wilayat Passport in week 3. Weekly “Restaurant of the Week” and Top 5 lists.

Review & expandWeeks 6 to 12

Check which areas and formats bring installs and restaurant views. Move budget to winners, expand listings in weaker regions, and prepare the next seasonal campaign (Ramadan or Khareef).

Suggested launch budget split

Share of whatever launch budget is set. Shift toward diners once supply targets are met.

Paid social · 30%
Creators · 20%
Owner acquisition · 20%
Search · 15%
Event & PR · 15%

Owner-side KPIs

Restaurants listed, profiles completed with menu + photos, regions covered, owner response time.

Diner-side KPIs

Installs, weekly active users, searches per user, restaurant page views, calls and direction taps.

Brand KPIs

Follower growth, saves and shares per post, branded search for “Dine Oman” and “داين عمان”.

Set numeric targets once the commercial model and budget are confirmed; none are invented here.

Before artwork goes final

  • Confirm the Arabic name (داين عُمان or مائدة عُمان) and run trademark, domain and handle checks.
  • Confirm the commercial model for owners; the ads here assume free listing at launch.
  • Commission real photography of listed restaurants; the plate graphics in this book are placeholders.
  • Hand the Arch D to a vector artist for optical refinement and a full export pack (SVG, PNG, app icon sizes).
  • Use real figures only (restaurant counts, followers, results) once they exist.
